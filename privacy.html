Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unbound DAO3 UB3 Ltd (RC 9983376), trading as UB3 Pay ("UB3 Pay", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the UB3 Pay mobile app and related services. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable Nigerian law.

UB3 Pay is currently in private testing. This policy applies to everyone who uses the app, including testers.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Unbound DAO3 UB3 Ltd is the data controller. Registered address: No. 151, Sabon Gida, Yahaya Gusau, Gwale Local Government, Kano, Kano State, Nigeria. Contact: ub3pay@ub3.org.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, your UB3 ID, and your profile photo if you add one.
  • Identity verification: your Bank Verification Number (BVN) and the details returned by the verification process, which we need before you can deposit or withdraw naira.
  • Wallet and transaction data: your wallet addresses, balances, transaction history, recipients you send to, saved favourites, and the bank account details you use for withdrawals.
  • Security data: information needed to protect your account, such as your transaction PIN settings, login activity and failed attempts.
  • Device and usage data: your device type, app version, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Support messages: messages and images you send to our customer support.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account and wallet, and to process deposits, withdrawals, transfers and swaps you request.
  • To verify your identity and meet legal duties on fraud prevention and anti-money laundering.
  • To protect the app and our customers, including detecting suspicious activity and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • To send service messages, such as transaction confirmations, security alerts and announcements.
  • To provide customer support, and to fix and improve the app.

We rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you, meeting our legal obligations, our legitimate interests in running and securing the service, and your consent where we ask for it (for example, for push notifications).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run UB3 Pay, and only as needed for that purpose. These include providers for payments and identity verification (such as Paystack), wallet infrastructure (such as Coinbase), authentication and database hosting (such as Google Firebase), server hosting, email delivery, and image storage. We may also disclose data when the law requires it, for example to courts, regulators or law enforcement, or to protect our rights and our customers.

Blockchain transactions are public by nature. Wallet addresses and transaction details you send on a public network can be seen by anyone and cannot be erased by us.

5. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our providers store or process data outside Nigeria. When this happens we take steps to make sure your data stays protected, as required by applicable law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, and for as long as the law requires us to keep financial and identity records. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

7. How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls, encryption in transit, and a transaction PIN to approve account actions. No system is completely secure, so please keep your PIN and device safe and tell us immediately if you think your account has been compromised.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of your data in a usable format, and withdraw consent you have given. Some of these rights are limited where we must keep records by law. To use your rights, write to ub3pay@ub3.org. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

UB3 Pay is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new date, and tell you in the app if the changes are important.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests: ub3pay@ub3.org.
