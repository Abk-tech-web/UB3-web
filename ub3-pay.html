UB3 Pay

One wallet for your naira and your digital assets. Built for customers in Nigeria.

What is UB3 Pay?

UB3 Pay is a mobile wallet app for individual adults in Nigeria. It lets you manage naira and digital assets, including dollar-pegged stablecoins such as USDT and USDC, from one place. UB3 Pay is a separate product from the UB3 research and education platform on this website.

What you will be able to do

Fund in naira

Add money to your wallet by bank transfer to your own dedicated account number.

Withdraw to your bank

Send naira from your wallet to your own bank account.

Send to other users

Send naira or digital assets to another UB3 Pay user with their UB3 ID.

Hold and swap digital assets

Hold, send and swap supported digital assets, including stablecoins, on supported networks.

How we keep accounts safe

  • Every customer is verified with their BVN before they can deposit or withdraw naira.
  • Every transaction is approved with a personal 4-digit PIN, and the app can be locked with the same PIN or biometrics.
  • Daily and per-transaction limits apply.
  • Customer support is available inside the app.

Availability and regulation

UB3 Pay is not yet publicly available. It is in private testing. Features that require regulatory approval will launch only after the required approvals or licensed partner arrangements are in place.

Digital assets can lose value and their prices can change quickly. Only use money you can afford to put at risk. Nothing on this page is investment advice.
